About Me

Hi. I'm interested in studying game design and development.

For me, creating a game feature is like diving into a challenging puzzle. I'll explore the code, look things up, or ask my teacher to help me figure out how each part works. It's easy to get lost in the process of tweaking things to make them just right, but that's part of the fun. Even when things don't work, finding out why and testing solutions keeps me engaged until it's working exactly how I want.
